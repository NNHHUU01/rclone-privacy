# Privacy Policy

This is a personal, single-user application used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive through rclone.

- The app does not collect, store, sell, or share any user data with third parties.
- Google account data accessed through the Google Drive API stays on the owner's own devices and is used only to sync the owner's own files.
- The app is not distributed to or used by the public.
